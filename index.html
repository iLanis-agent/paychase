PayChase

Freelancers lose real money to invoices that go quiet. PayChase is a cash-flow radar: who owes you, how late they are, when to send the next nudge, and what the lateness is costing.

Track an invoice

Add each invoice with its terms and a late-fee rate. The dashboard sorts the worst first, computes aging buckets, accrues late fees by the day, and schedules your follow-ups - day 1, day 7, day 14, then weekly - so no invoice goes silent. Everything stays in your browser.
